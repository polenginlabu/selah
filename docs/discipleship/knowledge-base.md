## WINNING — Helping someone find their way back to God

**Plan first**
- Plan ahead.
- Plan for productivity.
- If we have a goal, let them know.
- Plan for growth.

**Evaluation — WINNING**
1. Does my team know the plan and goals we have for our team and for the church?
2. When was the last time my team was involved in evangelism?
3. Are my new leaders and potential leaders engaging in the evangelism?
4. Are the upcoming leaders being trained?
5. What was the result of our last evangelism, what did we learn, and what can we improve?
6. How many people did we win this month?

## CONSOLIDATION — Helping someone start their new life with Jesus

*Consolidation is a family affair.*

**Principles in consolidation**
1. Personal connection.
2. Home visitation.
3. Community building — take them out of the usual.

**Evaluation — Consolidation**
1. Are our consolidation efforts consistent?
2. Am I getting to know the person I am following up?
3. Are the new believers being connected to the community?
4. Am I giving them a chance or platform to contribute?
5. When they contribute and engage, do I show my appreciation?
6. Did I invite them for the next meeting? Did I give them a good reason to come back?

## SENDING — Helping someone engage in Jesus' mission

1. Disciple deeper through coaching and mentoring.
   - Intentionality and intricacy.
   - Transformation.
2. Completion of the 12.
   - Complete your team.
   - Aligned — we do missions because we are sent by God.

### I AM SENT

- **John 20:21** — something must die in us first before we can be sent.
- **John 20:19** — what are the fears we have when doing the mission? The LORD is very intentional to place us. What is our mark? Our mark is a changed life. We are continuing the work of Christ. We are going because God is at work through us.

**Guidelines**
1. **We are not just going, we are being sent** (Romans 10:15).
   - Sending establishes authority.
   - God releases provisions.
   - Sending silences insecurities.
   - Our obedience is already a success for us.
2. **We do not just carry His message, but His presence** (Acts 1:8).
   - We prioritize hosting the Spirit of God.
   - Our greatest impact comes when we know who walks with us.
   - It attracts hunger to people.
   - The mission and vision are alive in us.
3. **We must die daily** — when we die daily, God lives in us (Luke 9:23).
   - Deny our self — set aside selfish interest.
   - Take up the cross daily — willingness to endure whatever may come; we do not question anymore.
   - Follow Me — believing, conforming, suffering.
4. **We are sent into a battle and victory is assured** (Luke 10:19) — expect resistance.

**Called and sent (Genesis 12:1-3, Romans 1:1)**
1. To be a servant — you belong to Christ.
2. Sent out (apostle / ekklesia).
3. Set apart or consecrated — not common; marked by purpose; living differently.

## Systems in the G12 vision

- **WIN (PAMPAPUNTA)** — help people find their way back to God.
- **CONSOLIDATE (PAMPABALIK)** — help people start their new life with Jesus.
- **DISCIPLE (PAMPALAGO)** — help people put their life back in order.
- **SEND (PAMPARAMI)** — help people engage in Jesus' mission.

**Benefits of setting up systems**
1. Conserve leadership capacity.
2. Ensure ministry continuity.
3. Improve ministry consistency.
4. Support sustainable growth.

### WINNING system

| Random winning | System-based winning |
| --- | --- |
| Invite when convenient | Weekly tracked invites |
| Random conversation | Defined prospect list |
| No clear target | Multiple entry points |
| Depends on mood or confidence | Intentional follow-through |

### CONSOLIDATION system

| Reactive follow-up | System-based consolidation |
| --- | --- |
| Occasional follow-up | 24–48hr follow-up |
| No clear ownership | Assigned owner per person |
| Assumes they will return | Clear next steps |
| | Relational integration — give a VIP kit |

### DISCIPLESHIP system

| Inconsistent discipleship | System-based discipleship |
| --- | --- |
| Attendance-only mindset | Clear growth pathway |
| Teaching based on inspiration | Weekly cell with application |
| No growth structure | Accountability |
| Passive learning | Intentional formation |

### SENDING system

| Convenience-driven sending | System-based sending |
| --- | --- |
| Wait until ready | Early delegation |
| Delay leadership exposure | Apprenticeship model |
| Fear of releasing | Progressive leadership exposure |
| Dependence on leader | Intentional sending |
